## Response snapshot

Fixed all three broken anchor link issues by updating the anchor references to match the actual heading IDs generated by Docusaurus:

1. **Cloud Options anchor** (2 occurrences in faq.mdx:67, 253):
   - Changed from `#cloud-options` to `#️-cloud-options-no-gpu-required`
   - The full heading "☁️ Cloud Options (No GPU Required)" generates an anchor that includes the emoji and full text

2. **Course Structure anchor** (2 occurrences):
   - Changed from `#course-structure` to `#-5-comprehensive-modules`
   - The intro.mdx file doesn't have a "Course Structure" heading, but has "📚 5 Comprehensive Modules"
   - Updated in module-5-capstone/module-5-assessment.mdx:934
   - Updated in module-5-capstone/week-16/chapter-32-deployment-future.mdx:1271

Docusaurus generates anchor IDs by converting headings to lowercase and replacing spaces with hyphens, but it also includes emojis and parenthetical text from the full heading.
